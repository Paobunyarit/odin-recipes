Lasagna

Ingredients

  • 1 (8 ounce) package lasagna noodles
  • 3 cups heavy cream
  • 2 (10.5 ounce) cans condensed cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ¼ cup butter
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ½ large onion, diced
  • >4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 5 mushrooms, diced
  • 1 cooked rotisserie chicken, shredded
  • alt and ground black pepper to taste
    • s
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 1 bunch fresh spinach, rinsed
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Directions

  1. Gather the ingredients.

    2. credit Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

  2. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add lasagna noodles and cook until al dente, about 8 to 10 minutes. Drain, and rinse with cold water.

    3. credit Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

  3. Mix heavy cream, cream of mushroom soup, Parmesan cheese, and butter in a saucepan over low heat; simmer, stirring frequently, until well blended.

    4. credit Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

  4. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook and stir onion in olive oil until tender, then add garlic and mushrooms.

    5. credit Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

  5. Mix in chicken, and cook until heated through. Season with salt and pepper.

    6. credit Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

  6. Lightly coat the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish with enough of the cream sauce mixture to coat.

    7. credit Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

  7. Layer with 1/3 of the lasagna noodles, 1/2 cup ricotta, 1/2 of the spinach.

    8. credit Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

  8. Add 1/2 the chicken mixture, and 1 cup mozzarella. Top with 1/3 the cream sauce mixture, and repeat the layers.

    9. credit Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

  9. Place the remaining noodles on top, and spread with remaining sauce.

    10. credit Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

  10. Bake 1 hour in the preheated oven, or until brown and bubbly. Top with the remaining mozzarella, and continue baking until cheese is melted and lightly browned.

    11. credit Dotdash Meredith Food Studios
